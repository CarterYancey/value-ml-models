# dataset_v1.5.1: the new features and labels in cell C (decisions 74 and 75)

2026-11-26. Carter asked for experiments with `dataset_v1.5.1`'s new
columns (the fundamental-history family, the widened trend family, the
fourteen 8-K counts) and its full-path labels, across models. The
program is decision 74 of [2026-11-26-decisions.md](2026-11-26-decisions.md);
its first reading and the follow-ups are decision 75. Every run here is
on `dataset_v1.5.1`, walk-forward, folds 2005–2020, trained on every
row and read inside the liquidity floor `dollar_volume_3m >= 100000`
on the portfolio screen (top 10 a quarter, at most 2 per sector, peers
in 5% bands of `log_marketcap_rank`). No v1.4.1 number appears here.

**How to read the tables.** `lead` is the screen's mean 3-year CAGR
minus its same-size peers', averaged over the 16 test years; `losers`
is the share of the screen's picks with a negative 3-year CAGR, beside
the peers'. The paired columns are the mean yearly gain over the
control with its standard error, and the years the arm is ahead. The
rule (decision 74.4): an arm is carried to three seeds when its gain
in lead is positive at about two standard errors, it is ahead in 10 or
more of 16 years, and no half is worse by more than its noise, on
both outcomes. p@20 ranks the arms as predictors of their label only.
